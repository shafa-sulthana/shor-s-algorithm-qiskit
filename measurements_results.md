# Quantum Measurement Results

## Experiment

A single qubit was placed into superposition using a Hadamard gate and measured 1000 times using Qiskit Aer Simulator.

## Results

| Measurement | Count | Probability |
|------------|------:|------------:|
| 0 | 468 | 46.8% |
| 1 | 532 | 53.2% |
| Total | 1000 | 100% |

## Conclusion

The results are approximately 50% for measuring 0 and 50% for measuring 1, which agrees with the expected behavior of a qubit in the |+> state.
